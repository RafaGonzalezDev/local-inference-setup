# 2026-08-06 - BTL-4 and BTL-4 Compact

**What**: BTL-4 35B Q4_K_M and BTL-4 Compact IQ2_XXS were incorporated into the Windows environment, with `text` and `agentic` profiles, launchers, pinned artifacts, and explicit reasoning separation. The `agentic` profile of Compact was subsequently adjusted to CPU-MoE 0 to also keep all MoE weights on GPU with its full 262,144-token window.

**Where**: `models\btl-4`, `models\btl-4-compact`, `config\models\btl-4*.psd1`, `config\catalog.psd1`, `scripts\models\btl-4*`, `scripts\common\Start-Llm.ps1`, `README.md`, and `docs\overview\models`.

**Why**: The two BTL-4 variants were needed in the canonical `D:\LLM` catalog: Q4_K_M prioritizes practical quality and Compact reduces the footprint to 9.97 GB. Both reuse the official runtime and the existing declarative engine, without duplicating logic per model. The RTX 5080 retained sufficient margin to eliminate the partial offload of four MoE layers to CPU in Compact.

**Provenance**: Compact uses the official GGUF `badtheorylabs/BTL-4-Compact`, revision `2a29bc80b0ebc80d6409fbad89f85d910dbb4aee`. Q4_K_M uses the conversion `DogukanUrker/BTL-4-GGUF`, revision `28921f57a52cb1a3bdd9b801162faa074c677118`, created from the checkpoint `badtheorylabs/BTL-4` without the non-existent MTP block.

**Validation**: Both SHA-256s passed two independent checks. The 28 catalog profiles generated valid plans. The `text` and `agentic` profiles of both models passed functional tests, including the 131,072 and 262,144-token windows. Both returned a structured tool call with the expected arguments, separated `reasoning_content`, and cleanly closed the server and port. Compact `agentic` passed again with CPU-MoE 0 in 4.05 seconds; during full loading the global GPU measurement was 14,771 of 16,303 MiB.

**Limitations**: Both builds are text-only and do not include vision or MTP. Brief functional tests do not substitute prolonged benchmarks of quality, speed, or stability.
